The Thinkers’ Match

No score. No clock. Only the beat.

Kick off

Eleven great minds, one very heavy ball. Cream against navy. Pick any of the eleven below (tap a face, press their number, or Q/E) and play as them.

Every eight beats the conditions change on their own, or tap a condition to jump in.

☕ Cooling break

Everyone catches their breath. The ball can wait.

Move: WASD / arrows · Kick: Space (hold to charge) · or drag on the pitch and use the Kick button · Switch player: tap a face, keys 1–9, 0 (=10), – (=11), or Q/E
